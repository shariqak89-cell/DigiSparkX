Our Mission

Creating opportunity through practical digital skills.

DigiSparkX is more than a digital skills training initiative; it is a platform dedicated to creating meaningful opportunities.

Our mission is to empower young people, particularly students from underprivileged and underserved communities, by equipping them with the confidence, creativity, digital competencies, and leadership skills required to become self-reliant and independent individuals.

We believe that talent exists everywhere, but access to opportunity does not. DigiSparkX exists to reduce that gap by giving learners practical exposure, real guidance, creative confidence, and modern digital skills.

Many students are already reaching good positions and securing jobs through digital platforms, marketplaces and commerce ecosystems such as Amazon and Meesho. Our purpose is to help more learners become capable enough to use these opportunities with confidence and independence.

My Education

I am currently pursuing my B.A. English Honours from the University of Delhi, and I am a first-year student. Alongside my education, I am also working on my creative and digital skills. Managing college, studies, creative projects, content creation, AI learning and other responsibilities together has not always been easy. My Journey with DigiSparkX

I started working with Sir in April 2026, and later joined DigiSparkX in June 2026 as a Founder Member. With continuous practice, learning and guidance, I slowly started understanding them.

The biggest thing I learned was not only a software or a technical skill. I learned to believe in my ability to learn. Every project gave me experience. Every mistake taught me something. Every failure pushed me to improve.

My YouTube Journey

Alongside my learning journey, I also started building my own YouTube presence through Fabiha Afroz. My YouTube journey has been full of ups and downs. My Parents & My Motivation

What I Have Learned

This journey has taught me that success is not always about views, followers, likes or recognition. Sometimes success is learning something new, trying again after failure, creating when nobody is watching, managing responsibilities when it feels difficult, and making your parents proud.

My Vision

My vision is to continue growing as a creative professional, digital creator and lifelong learner. I want to continue exploring Artificial Intelligence, Creative AI, Graphic Design, Logo Making, Branding, Video Editing, Digital Marketing, Social Media, Content Creation and emerging digital technologies.

I am still a learner. I am still making mistakes. I am still figuring things out. But I am no longer afraid of starting from zero, because every expert was once a beginner.
